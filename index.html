ABOUT US

Supporting Our Community Since 1986

Brockman House is a not for profit community based neighborhood community centre, child care and out of school hours care service. We aim to provide support for our local community and families in need in a way that fosters self sufficiency and empowers people in meeting their needs.

V

Vision

A Healthy, Inclusive and Empowered Community.

M

Mission

To provide support and services to individuals and families in a way that fosters self-sufficiency and strengthens community connection.

C

Child Safe Commitment

We provide a child-safe environment where children feel respected, supported and empowered to grow.
